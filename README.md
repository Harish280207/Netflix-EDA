# 🎬 Netflix EDA — Exploratory Data Analysis

### 📊 An Exploratory Data Analysis of Netflix Movies & TV Shows

This project performs an in-depth **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the Netflix titles dataset to uncover patterns and trends across movies and TV shows available on Netflix.

The analysis focuses on **data cleaning, statistical exploration, visualization, content trends, ratings, genres, countries, release years, and other key characteristics** of the Netflix catalog.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

With thousands of movies and TV shows available on Netflix, analyzing the catalog can reveal interesting insights about:

- 🎥 Movies vs. TV Shows
- 📅 Content released over the years
- 🌍 Content distribution by country
- ⭐ Ratings and audience classification
- 🎭 Popular genres and categories
- ⏱️ Movie duration
- 📺 TV show seasons
- 👤 Directors and cast
- 🔎 Patterns and trends in Netflix content

The goal of this project is to transform raw Netflix data into **meaningful insights through data analysis and visualization**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The major objectives of this project are:

1. Understand and clean the Netflix dataset.
2. Analyze the distribution of Movies and TV Shows.
3. Identify content trends over the years.
4. Explore the most common genres.
5. Analyze content distribution across countries.
6. Study Netflix ratings and classifications.
7. Analyze movie durations and TV show seasons.
8. Identify patterns in the Netflix catalog.
9. Create meaningful visualizations to communicate insights.
10. Draw conclusions from the exploratory analysis.

## 📊 Exploratory Data Analysis

The project explores multiple dimensions of the Netflix catalog.

### 🎬 Movies vs. TV Shows

Analysis of the proportion of Movies and TV Shows available in the dataset.

### 📅 Release Year Analysis

Examining how Netflix content has changed across different years and identifying periods of increased content production.

### 🌍 Country Analysis

Exploring the countries contributing content to the Netflix catalog.

### 🎭 Genre Analysis

Identifying frequently occurring genres and examining the diversity of Netflix content.

### ⭐ Rating Analysis

Studying the distribution of Netflix titles across different ratings and audience classifications.

### ⏱️ Duration Analysis

For movies, the analysis examines runtime patterns.

For TV shows, the analysis explores the number of seasons.
